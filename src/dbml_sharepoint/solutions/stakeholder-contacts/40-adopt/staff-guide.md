# Stakeholder contacts: staff guide

## The three-minute habit

Had a meeting, call or meaningful email exchange with an external contact?
Before you move on:

1. **SC_Interaction** → **New**. The form is three sections and they run
   in that order.
2. *What happened*: a **Title** that a colleague could skim (for example,
   "Quarterly review: funding timeline discussed"), the **Contact** (add
   them first if new, 30 seconds, below), the date and the kind. The form
   refuses a date in the future: this list is a record, not a calendar.
3. *What was said*: **Summary**: what was discussed, what was agreed,
   what was *promised*, by either side. Promises are the thing people
   forget and stakeholders don't.
4. *Our record*: **Recorded by**: you.

That's it. The payoff: before your next meeting with anyone, open *By
contact* and open their group. Walking in knowing the history is the whole
game. It is grouped rather than filtered, and it holds the whole thread
however old. The *Recent activity* view is the last ninety days, and this
one is everything.

## Adding organisations and contacts

- New organisation: **SC_Organisation** first: type, website, and crucially
  an **Owner** (the person here who owns the relationship). An organisation
  with no owner falls into the blank group at the end of *By owner*, which
  is where the quarterly check finds it; do not let yours be the one that
  sits there.
- New person: **SC_Contact**: name, organisation, role, work email/phone.
  **Professional facts only** in Notes ("prefers phone", "works Tuesdays–
  Thursdays"), never opinions or personal details; they'd be visible to
  the whole team and possibly to the contact themselves under privacy law.
  The form header says exactly that, every time it opens, because that is
  the moment it matters.

## When people move on

Untick **Active**. Don't delete. Their interaction history is the
organisational memory; the flag just takes them out of the working views
and flips their chip from *Active* to *Moved on*. They appear in the
*Moved on* view, which is where the annual privacy purge clears contact
details while keeping the row and its thread. If they resurface at a new
organisation, create a new contact row there.

## What NOT to do

- Don't hoard: if it's not in the register, the relationship is a personal
  possession, and possessions leave with people.
- Don't paste email chains into Summary. Synthesise, then link if needed.
- Don't record sensitive personal information. Ever. See the governance
  doc's privacy rules.
