# Meeting actions: staff guide

## The 5-minute habit (minute-taker or chair)

During or straight after each meeting:

1. **MA_Meeting** -> **New**. *The meeting*: "Forum, date" title, type,
   date, chair. *The record*: link the minutes doc if you keep one.
   (30 seconds.)
2. For each decision made: **MA_Decision** -> **New**, pick the meeting,
   state the decision *as a decision*, "We will X from Y", with just
   enough in *Why* that nobody has to relitigate it in six months.
3. For each action: **MA_ActionItem** -> **New**, pick the meeting, a verb
   for a title, then *Owner and date*: **one owner** (never "team"), a
   real due date. Both are required; the form will not let you skip
   either, which is the point.

That's the whole system. No minutes-formatting marathon required. Many
teams find the three lists *replace* long-form minutes for routine meetings.

## Between meetings (everyone)

- Your queue is ***My actions***. It follows whoever is signed in, so it
  is your list without you filtering anything. Update Status as you work.
- Done: set Status **Done**. The **Completed Date** field appears when you
  do, and the form will not save without it. An action finished on a date
  nobody can name is still In progress.
- Can't deliver by the due date? Change the date and say why in Notes.
  A moved date with a reason beats a silently overdue action. Until you
  do, the date is red with a warning icon everywhere it appears.
- An action that no longer makes sense -> **Dropped** with a note. Nothing
  refuses that save, deliberately: dropping honestly is already better
  than leaving it Open forever, and the form is not going to make the
  honest move the harder one.

## Opening the next meeting

First agenda item: the *By meeting* view, opened at the **previous**
meeting's group. It shows every action from that meeting including the
Done and Dropped ones, which is what "did anyone do them?" actually asks.
Two minutes, and suddenly actions get done, because everyone knows this
view is coming.

## What NOT to do

- Don't assign actions to people not in the meeting without telling them.
- Don't reword a Decision after the fact. Record a superseding decision at
  the meeting that changed it.
- Don't delete anything; Dropped/Done rows are the team's memory.
