# Visitor log: staff guide

## Signing someone in (reception or the host, 30 seconds)

1. **VI_Visit** → **New** (or the visitor uses the kiosk tablet).
2. **Who is visiting**: name, organisation, **type**, purpose.
3. **On site**: **host** (the staff member responsible for them),
   location, **Signed In At** = now, badge ref if you issue them.
   *Signed Out At* isn't on the New form: nobody signs out on arrival.
4. **Induction**: this section only appears for **Contractors** and
   **Students / placements**. Tick *Induction sighted* only after actually
   sighting it: the tick is the record that site induction happened
   before work started. No induction, no work; call the host.

The form refuses a sign-in dated in the future and tells you why. It
cannot check that the host exists or that the sign-out is later than the
sign-in: those are habits, not rules; see governance.

## Signing out: the half everyone forgets

When your visitor leaves, open their row and set **Signed Out At**. Until
you do, their *Signed Out At* cell reads **On site** in blue in every
view, which is exactly what it means.

Hosts: your guests are yours to sign out; reception can't see them leave.
The **Never signed out** view gets tidied each morning: a phantom visitor
on the muster list is a warden searching a building for someone who left
at 3 pm yesterday.

## The evacuation moment (wardens)

At the assembly point, open **VI_Visit** on any phone. It opens on **On
site now**, the muster list: name, organisation, type, host,
location, purpose. Check people off against reality; the host of anyone
unaccounted-for is standing next to you and knows where they were headed.
This is the sixty seconds the whole register exists for, and it's why the
sign-out habit matters every ordinary day.

## The other views

- **Signed in today**: the day's traffic, newest first.
- **Contractors on site**: who is working on site right now, with their
  induction status beside them.
- **Never signed out**: the morning tidy-up queue.

## What NOT to do

- Don't sign in groups as "Smith + 4": every person is a row; the muster
  list can't check off a plus-four.
- Don't record more than the register needs: no visitor phone numbers or
  personal details beyond name and organisation (see the privacy rules in
  governance).
- Don't back-date a sign-in to tidy the record. Back-fill a *sign-out* and
  say so in the row; a sign-in time is evidence of when someone was in the
  building.
