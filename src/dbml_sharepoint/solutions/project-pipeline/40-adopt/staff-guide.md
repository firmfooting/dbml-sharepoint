# Project pipeline — staff guide

## Proposing (anyone — 5 minutes)

Got an idea that needs more than your own team's Tuesday afternoon?

1. **PP_Proposal** → **New**. The form asks for **The idea** and, if you
   already know them, the **Scoping** ratings. It does not ask you to name
   a sponsor, and it does not show you the decision fields — those arrive
   when there is something to record.
2. **Title**: outcome first — "Digitise visitor sign-in across all sites",
   not "Sign-in project".
3. **Problem**: the problem as the people who have it would describe it.
   If you can't write this paragraph, the idea isn't ready — that's fine;
   write what you can and leave it at **Idea**.
4. **Outcome**: what's different if it works. Numbers where honest.
5. Save. Ideas are welcome half-formed — the pipeline's job is to grow the
   good ones and give the rest a dignified written burial. An unscored idea
   shows a **blank** score, not a low one: nobody has assessed it yet, and
   the register says so rather than guessing.

You can keep editing your proposal through Scoping — refine it as you learn.

## Scoping (proposer + a sponsor)

An Idea moves to **Scoping** when an executive agrees to sponsor the
question (not the project — the question). Together you set:

- **Benefit** and **Feasibility**, honestly, against the governance
  definitions — the score computes itself, and gaming it is self-defeating
  because everyone can see the inputs;
- **CostBand** — order of magnitude, not a quote;
- then Stage → **Ready for decision**.

The list will refuse that last step until Benefit, Feasibility and Cost
Band are all set. An unscored proposal cannot be ranked, so it would sit at
the bottom of an agenda sorted by score — which is the same as not being on
it.

## The gate (decision-makers)

The *Decision queue* view, sorted by score, is the agenda — with Benefit,
Feasibility and Cost Band beside the score, because overruling the score
out loud is only possible if everyone can see what it was computed from.
Each proposal leaves the gate as **Approved** (with the sponsor confirmed),
**Declined** (with why — future-you will thank you), or **Parked** (real
"not now", with what would un-park it). Set the stage and the **Decision
date** and **Decision notes** appear on the form; the save is refused
without the date.

## Delivery

Approved work moves **In delivery** → **Delivered**. The **Delivered date**
appears when you set that stage, and the save is refused without it. The
pipeline doesn't replace project management — it replaces the mystery of
what was ever agreed and why.

## The five views, and what each is for

Deployed with the list — there is nothing to build by hand.

| View | Use it when |
|---|---|
| **The funnel** *(default)* | The whole live pipeline, grouped by stage, ranked by score inside each group |
| **Decision queue** | The monthly gate agenda |
| **Portfolio** | The quarterly capacity read — approved and in-delivery work, grouped by sponsor |
| **Graveyard** | The annual read-through: what we declined and parked, and why |
| **Delivered** | The evidence that any of it was worth doing |
