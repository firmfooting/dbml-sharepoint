# Managing a shared-services provider

An organisation that gets its ICT, or any other service, from a shared
provider tends to know less about that arrangement than either side
assumes. Three families cover it, in the order the questions come up.

## Start with what you depend on

`seam-register` is a six-week exercise, not a standing register. Two people
map what the provider runs, what the organisation runs itself, and where the
joins are, one row per service with evidence against every row. The output
is a seam list: every place two sources disagree or nobody answers, with an
owner to resolve it. Run it once, hand over the one-page map, and keep the
register for the second pass.

## Then keep the record of how they perform

`service-evidence-register` is the standing log that follows. Every event
where the provider did or did not do what was expected is logged when it
happens, chased with dates, and raised as a theme when it repeats. The seams
the first exercise found are the themes to watch first.

## And the agreement they perform against

`contract-register` holds the services agreement, the schedule and
every other instrument the provider's obligations come from, with their
renewal dates. The seam register's document asks are usually the first time
anyone has looked for these; once found, this is where they live.
