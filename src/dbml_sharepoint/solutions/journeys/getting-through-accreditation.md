# Getting through accreditation

`compliance-obligations` is the spine: every legislative, accreditation and
funding obligation with an owner, its evidence and a review date.
`audit-actions` carries recommendations to closure against a stated
closure-evidence standard.

The rest are what an assessor asks to see. `declarations-register` holds
interests and gifts, `delegations-register` mirrors the instrument of
delegation, and `contract-register` and `grants-register` carry the agreements
and the post-award obligations most organisations drop.
`research-ethics-register-simple` applies where projects are referred to a
partner HREC, and `records-digitisation` answers whether a digitised record may
be kept on a platform at all, rather than merely stored there.
