# Replacing the paper books

Start at the front desk. `visitor-log`'s default view is the evacuation
muster list, live at the desk, and it pays back in the first week without a
policy decision. `routine-checks` replaces the checklists taped to walls:
every fridge reading and trolley check is timestamped, attributed and marked
in or out of range.

`asset-register` and `training-register` answer the questions an auditor
asks first: what do we own and who holds it, and who is covered for what
until when. `contract-register` does the same for agreements, with the
expiring and auto-renewing contracts in views of their own.

Deploy `deployment-log` once you are deploying to more than one site. It
records what this tool did rather than what the organisation decided: every
other deploy stamps it with what was provisioned, where, by whom and whether
the run finished.
