# Looking after the estate

`asset-register` holds equipment and IT assets with unique tags, a location
and who currently has them. `equipment-maintenance` carries the testing and
preventive schedule with evidence-linked history, and its Overdue view is meant
to be empty.

`vehicle-log` replaces the pool-car books, calculating kilometres from odometer
readings. Its Purpose column is the substantiation a fringe-benefits review
asks for.
