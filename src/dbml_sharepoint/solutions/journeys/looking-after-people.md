# Looking after people

`onboarding-tracker` coordinates the HR, IT, facilities and finance tasks a
new starter needs, from one record. `training-register` holds the course
catalogue and per-person records with their expiry dates.

`credentialing-register` is the stricter form for practitioners: who may do
what, on whose decision, until when, and with what evidence.
`volunteer-register` keeps police and working-with-children checks in date, and
ships with no general access.
