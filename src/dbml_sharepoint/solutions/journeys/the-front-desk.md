# The front desk

`visitor-log`'s default view is the evacuation muster list, live at the desk.
`switchboard-log` is the three paper books digitised: the code log with its
calculated duration, the message book with its relay times, and the key
register.

`service-requests` is where the desk sends work belonging to facilities, IT or
admin, so a request stops being a sticky note. All three pay back in the first
week and need no policy decision first.
