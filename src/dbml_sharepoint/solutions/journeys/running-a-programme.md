# Running a programme

A programme fails in three places at once, and no single register covers all
three. Delivery drifts, which a RAID log catches. Accountabilities rot, which a
RACI matrix catches. And the changes that actually unblock the work are often
made by somebody else's team, which neither catches.

This family is all three on one site, because they are read together: an issue
is usually a request that has sat with a provider for three weeks, and the
person who can escalate it is whoever the accountability register names.

The two halves were once separate templates, a project RAID log and a standing
RACI matrix. They were retired in favour of this one: keeping three families in
step meant making the same change three times, and the merged family is what
each of them was for anyway. A project too small for ten lists can deploy this
and leave the lists it does not need empty.
