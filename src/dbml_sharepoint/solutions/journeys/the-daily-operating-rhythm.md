# The daily operating rhythm

`tiered-huddle` is the huddle board itself: one row per day per tier, one
column per stream, and a blank cell that means unreported rather than fine.
Streams can be added or retired without losing the history behind them.

`routine-checks` digitises the checklists that fill it, the fridge
temperatures and trolley checks and rounds, each one timestamped and
attributed to whoever did it.
