# Starting a project

`raid-log` is the project meeting's agenda: risks, actions, issues and
decisions read together rather than in four places. `raci-matrix` answers who
is accountable when one of them goes wrong, with exactly one Accountable per
row and a re-confirmation cadence driven by criticality.

`meeting-actions` carries what the meeting itself produced. `stakeholder-contacts`
holds the external organisations and people the project has to keep informed. A
risk that outgrows the project graduates to `risk-register`.
