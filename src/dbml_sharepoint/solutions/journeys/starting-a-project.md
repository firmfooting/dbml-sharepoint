# Starting a project

`programme-governance` is the project meeting's agenda and its accountability
record on one site: risks, actions, issues and decisions read together rather
than in four places, each workstream's activities carrying exactly one
Responsible and one Accountable, and a queue for the tenant-level changes
somebody else's team has to make.

It is ten lists, which is more than a small project needs on day one. Deploy
it and use the four delivery lists; the rest cost nothing until you have
something to put in them.

`meeting-actions` carries what the meeting itself produced.
`stakeholder-contacts` holds the external organisations and people the project
has to keep informed. A risk that outgrows the project graduates to
`risk-register`.
