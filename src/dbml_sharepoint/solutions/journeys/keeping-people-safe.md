# Keeping people safe

`incident-management` records corporate incidents and the corrective actions
that close them, with a report-only permission level so anybody can raise one.
`complaints-feedback` carries two calculated response clocks and ships with no
general access.

`risk-register` rates on a self-rating 5x5 matrix where a matrix-inconsistent
entry cannot be saved at all. `service-evidence-register` is the
contemporaneous record of what a provider actually delivered, where how
promptly the record was made is itself a column.

Read the statutory boundary before deploying any of them: a SharePoint list
never replaces a mandated clinical or incident system.
