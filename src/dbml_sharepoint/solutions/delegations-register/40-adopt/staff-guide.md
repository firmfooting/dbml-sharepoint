# Delegations register: staff guide

## Checking your authority (ten seconds, before you sign)

Open **DG_Delegation** -> the **By role** view -> find your role in the
group list -> expand it. That's what you may approve, to what limit, under
what conditions. Before signing anything you haven't signed before (a
contract, a hire, an invoice above your usual) the check is ten seconds
and the alternative is an unauthorised approval with your name on it.

The view is already there; you don't build it and you shouldn't rename it.
It shows **current** delegations only, which is the point: superseded
authority in a "what may I approve" answer is worse than no answer.

Three rules of reading:

1. **The role holds the authority, not you personally.** Acting in a role
   (formally) means holding its delegations; being "basically the acting
   manager" (informally) means holding nothing. See governance for
   acting arrangements.
2. **Limits are per the wording.** "Up to $50,000 per transaction" does
   not mean two $30,000 halves. Splitting a commitment to fit a
   delegation is the classic breach, and it's visible in hindsight every
   time.
3. **Conditions are part of the authority.** A two-signatory condition
   unmet means the approval didn't happen, however confident the one
   signature felt.

## The four views

| View | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **By area** | The default. Current delegations grouped by area, the working lookup. |
| **By role** | Current delegations grouped by role: "what can this role approve?" |
| **Reviews due** | Current delegations due for review inside the next ninety days. Coordinators' queue. |
| **History** | Superseded delegations, most recently approved first, with the supersession note. This is how an auditor reads an approval made three years ago. |

## When the register and reality disagree

If your instrument says one thing and the register another, the
**instrument wins** (the link at the top of every delegation form opens
it) and tell a Governance Coordinator immediately so the transcription is
fixed. If you think a delegation is *wrong* (too
low, missing, outdated), that's a change to the instrument: raise it
through the change-register; coordinators can't edit authority into
existence.

## For approvers of other registers

The change-register's authority table, grants' bid approvals and the
project pipeline's gates all resolve here: when those documents say "per
your delegations", this register is the lookup. Recording an approval?
Note the role you approved under: future-you at audit time says thanks.
