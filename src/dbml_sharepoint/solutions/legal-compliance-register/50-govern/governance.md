# Legal compliance register: governance

## Roles and handoffs

| Role | Assignment | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Platform owner | `Document.PlatformOwner` | Upload a new SAQ, assign the division executive, chase progress and record the completed assessment in the compliance platform |
| Executive | `Document.ExecutiveResponsible` | Assign a business owner and decide whether to review before recording |
| Business owner | `Document.BusinessOwner` | Complete the Excel SAQ and date its completion |

Platform owners belong to **LC Compliance Coordinators**. Executives and
business owners belong to **LC Assessment Owners**. The existing group names
are retained. Assessment Owners can edit without delete; coordinators maintain
the library. The roles are a working procedure, not separate permissions on
status values. Review assignments and file history when auditing handoffs.

## Assessment and reference records

A new assessment always gets a new file and assessment reference. Earlier
assessments keep their assignments and completed work when responsibility
changes. Update open assessments deliberately; folder membership does not
automatically update the person columns. The executive delegates each new
assessment rather than inheriting a business owner from a topic register.

The publisher's issuance year and quarter are independent of the assessment
cycle. An older publisher issue may support a later assessment. Retain the
issue label and portal topic ID so reporting can associate the exact sources.
REGs are references and are excluded from every assessment worklist.

## Optional executive review

The executive chooses **Review requirement** when delegating. *Not required*
lets a completed SAQ go directly to the recording worklist. *Required* holds
it in **Awaiting review** until **Reviewed date** is entered. Review is not
an unconditional approval gate. The completion date belongs to the business
owner; the review date belongs to the executive.

## Data quality

The declared SAQ save rule requires:

- Topic name, assessment reference, division and due date when *In progress*
  or *Complete*.
- A completion date when *Complete*.
- *Complete* and a completion date before entering a review or recording date.
- A review date before recording when review is requested.
- Completion, review and recording dates that are not in the future.

REGs bypass the assessment rule. The separate issuance-year rule accepts
blank values or a four-digit year, including publisher files older than 2024.
A cancelled assessment does not need a due date simply to record cancellation.

Person assignments are governance checks: the platform owner checks the
executive and platform-owner fields; the executive checks the business owner.
The existing live library probes show that bare uploads can leave metadata
empty, so an upload succeeding is not proof that these checks passed.
The template's save rule does not attempt to validate person columns.

Use **Pending** for missing assignments and late work, **Awaiting review**
for requested reviews, and **To record in the portal** for completed work
still needing entry in the compliance platform. Agree the review cadence and
recording turnaround locally; the template does not invent a fixed quarterly
assessment cycle or a mandatory executive sign-off.

## Reporting and content ownership

The generated reporting pack exposes the Document library's tracking metadata.
Power Query or Power BI can separately read the Excel files for question-level
responses, compliance, risk, controls, gaps and evidence. That workbook-content
query is organisation-specific and is not generated by this template.

Do not duplicate those contents in library columns or tracking notes. Associate
reporting by portal topic ID, publisher issue and assessment reference, retaining
the source file identity. A topic ID alone does not distinguish assessments.
The former cross-list `OwnerDrift` calculation no longer applies.

## Retention and migration

Keep completed and cancelled assessment files. Agree retention with records
governance and apply the appropriate library settings during adoption.
Version 2 creates a new library and does not migrate version 1 automatically.
Reconcile source files, tracking data and required history before retiring
old containers. Never use rollback to migrate or clean up production records.
