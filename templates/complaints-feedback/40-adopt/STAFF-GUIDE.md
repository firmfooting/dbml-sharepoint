# Complaints & feedback — staff guide

## Recording feedback (front-line recorders — 3 minutes)

Someone gives you a complaint, compliment or suggestion — in person, by
phone, however:

1. **CF_Feedback** → **New**.
2. **What was raised**: **Title**, neutral and factual — "Complaint -
   waiting time at reception, 14 July". No names in the title. Then
   **Type**, **Channel**, **Received date**, and **Detail**: what they
   actually said, in substance, in their terms. Don't editorialise, don't
   defend, don't diagnose. Use initials or roles for third parties.
3. **Triage**: set **Severity** if you can judge it; the handler confirms
   it against the definitions in governance.
4. Save. You can't edit afterwards — what was recorded at the time is
   evidential. Forgot something? Tell the handler; they append, dated.

The New form doesn't ask you for a handler, an acknowledgement, an outcome
or a closure. Those aren't yours, so they aren't on your form.

**Tell the person what happens next**: someone will acknowledge their
feedback and a named handler will respond. That sentence, said at the
counter, is half of complaint handling.

## Handling feedback (handlers)

The list opens on **Open by handler** — everything not yet closed, grouped
by handler and collapsed, oldest first. Your group is your caseload.

1. **Triage** view daily: everything still at *Received*. Confirm
   **Severity** against the definitions, set yourself (or the right
   person) as **Handler**.
2. **Acknowledge fast** — contact the person, tell them who is handling
   it, set **Acknowledged Date**. The **Unacknowledged** view is
   everything still waiting for that call, oldest first; it is the SLA
   most organisations fail first and the only one the complainant
   experiences directly. The list will not let you move an item past
   *Received* without the date.
3. Investigate; append findings to Detail (dated, under the original).
4. Respond to the person. Set Status **Responded** and **Outcome**
   appears — record it honestly (upheld / partially / not upheld).
5. Close it. Set Status **Closed**; **Closed Date** and **Learning**
   appear. The list refuses the save without an Outcome and a Closed Date.
   **Learning** it cannot enforce — fill it in anyway. One sentence
   minimum; "nothing to change" is an acceptable answer only if it's true.

Both day-counts calculate themselves and draw as bars, coloured by the
item's **Severity** rather than by the number — so three days is green on
a Standard item and red on a Critical one, and you don't have to hold the
SLA table in your head.

## The two review views

- **Closed last 30 days** — grouped by type, with both clocks and the
  outcome. This is the monthly pack's raw material. It is a *rolling*
  thirty days, not a calendar month, and it carries no totals; see
  governance before you quote a figure from it.
- **The learning shelf** — every closed item, newest first, with what
  changed because of it in the row. This is the one that matters.

## Compliments count too

Record them, close them "No response required" (or thank the person), and
route them to the team concerned. Trend data without the good news is just
a misery index.
