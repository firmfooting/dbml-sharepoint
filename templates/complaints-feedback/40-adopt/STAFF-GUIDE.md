# Complaints & feedback — staff guide

## Recording feedback (front-line recorders — 3 minutes)

Someone gives you a complaint, compliment or suggestion — in person, by
phone, however:

1. **CF_Feedback** → **New**.
2. **Title**: neutral, factual — "Complaint - waiting time at reception,
   14 July". No names in the title.
3. **Type**, **Received date**, **Channel**.
4. **Detail**: what they actually said, in substance, in their terms. Don't
   editorialise, don't defend, don't diagnose. Use initials or roles for
   third parties.
5. Save. You can't edit afterwards — what was recorded at the time is
   evidential. Forgot something? Tell the handler; they append, dated.

**Tell the person what happens next**: someone will acknowledge their
feedback and a named handler will respond. That sentence, said at the
counter, is half of complaint handling.

## Handling feedback (handlers)

1. **Triage** view daily: confirm **Severity** against the definitions, set
   yourself (or the right person) as **Handler**.
2. **Acknowledge fast** — contact the person, tell them who is handling it,
   set **AcknowledgedDate**. The acknowledgement clock is the SLA most
   organisations fail first.
3. Investigate; append findings to Detail (dated, under the original).
4. Respond to the person; record **Outcome** honestly (upheld / partially /
   not upheld), set **ClosedDate**, Status **Closed**.
5. Fill **Learning** — one sentence minimum. "Nothing to change" is an
   acceptable answer only if it's true.

## Compliments count too

Record them, close them "No response required" (or thank the person), and
route them to the team concerned. Trend data without the good news is just
a misery index.
