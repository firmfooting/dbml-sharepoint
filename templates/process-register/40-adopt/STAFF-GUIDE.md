# Process register — filling the inventory

*The register is built in workshops, not by decree. This guide is for the
facilitator and every team that owns processes.*

## The inventory workshop (60–90 minutes per function)

Get the team that actually runs the work in a room with the register open:

1. **List the processes** first, no detail: "what are the things this team
   does repeatedly that involve a form, an approval, a register, or a
   handover?" Titles go in as you talk — verb first ("Approve staff
   travel"). Expect 10–25 per function; if you have 60, you're listing
   tasks, not processes — merge up.
2. **Second pass, per process** (3 minutes each, timeboxed):
   - **Owner** — one name. "The team" is not an owner.
   - **CurrentState** — with total honesty. The register is a map, not a
     performance review; a spreadsheet named as a spreadsheet is a good
     answer.
   - **Criticality** and **PainLevel** against the definitions on the wall
     (from the governance doc). The score computes itself.
   - **PainNotes** — one concrete sentence: "re-keyed three times",
     "only Dana knows how", "audit finding 2025".
3. Don't solve anything in the workshop. The register's job today is to
   *see*; solving comes ranked, later.

## After the workshop (owners)

- Fill **Purpose** for your rows (two sentences).
- Sanity-check your scores against other teams' — the facilitator will
  flag outliers.

## As digitisation proceeds

The team doing the digitising updates **DigitisationStatus** and, when a
process lands in its new home, links it (**SystemUrl**) and sets the state
to its new truth. **Digitised** means running-in-the-new-way — not
built-and-ignored (the governance doc defines done).

## Keeping it alive

New process? Add it when it becomes repeatable. Process retired? Status
stays, PainNotes say "retired — absorbed into X". The inventory's value
compounds only if it stays truthful — see the review cadence in governance.
