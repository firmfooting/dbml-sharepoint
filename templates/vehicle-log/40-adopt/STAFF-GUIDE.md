# Vehicle log — drivers' guide

*30 seconds when you take the car, 30 seconds when you bring it back.
That's the whole deal.*

## Taking a car

1. Scan the QR on the key tag (or open **VE_Trip** → **New**).
2. Pick the **vehicle**; you're the driver by default.
3. **Purpose**: destination and business reason — "Warragul clinic —
   equipment delivery". This line is the organisation's tax evidence;
   "errands" and "meeting" don't substantiate anything. Specific beats
   long.
4. **OdoStart**: read the actual odometer. Not the trip meter, not a
   guess — the reading. **DepartedAt** = now. Save. Drive.

## Bringing it back

Open your trip (the *Out now* view finds it fastest): **OdoEnd** from the
odometer, **ReturnedAt** = now. Kilometres calculate themselves. Fuel
added, damage spotted, warning lights → **Notes** — the next driver reads
it, and so does the workshop.

If TripKm shows **negative**, one of your readings is wrong — fix it now
while you're standing at the car, not at month-end.

## Private or commute use

Only if your organisation permits it (check governance) and only recorded
as its own trip with the honest `TripType`. Mixed trips are two rows.
The tax value of the whole log rests on the business/private split being
truthful — one creative entry taints the book.

## Forgot to log?

It happens. Log it as soon as you remember, reconstruct the odometer from
the surrounding trips (the *Per vehicle* view shows the gap), and say
"logged late" in Notes. The gap-with-honesty rule applies here as
everywhere: a marked late entry is fine; a fabricated tidy one is not.
