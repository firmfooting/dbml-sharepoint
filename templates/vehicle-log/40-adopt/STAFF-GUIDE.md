# Vehicle log — drivers' guide

*30 seconds when you take the car, 30 seconds when you bring it back.
That's the whole deal, and the form is built around it.*

## Taking a car

1. Scan the QR on the key tag (or open **VE_Trip** → **New**).
2. **The trip**: pick the **vehicle**; you're the driver by default.
   **Trip type**, and **Purpose** — destination and business reason,
   "Warragul clinic — equipment delivery". This line is the
   organisation's tax evidence; "errands" and "meeting" don't substantiate
   anything. Specific beats long.
3. **Out and back**: **Departed At** = now, **Odometer Start** — read the
   actual odometer. Not the trip meter, not a guess — the reading. Save.
   Drive.

The New form doesn't ask for the return half, because you don't know it
yet. It appears when you come back.

## Bringing it back

The list opens on **Out now** — every trip with no return recorded, which
is also the fastest way to find yours. Open it: **Odometer End** from the
odometer, **Returned At** = now. Kilometres calculate themselves. Fuel
added, damage spotted, warning lights → **Notes** — the next driver reads
it, and so does the workshop.

The list won't let you save a returned trip without the closing odometer.

If **Trip km** shows **negative**, one of your readings is wrong — and the
whole row goes pink in the **By vehicle** view so nobody can miss it. Fix
it now while you're standing at the car, not at month-end.

## Private or commute use

Only if your organisation permits it (check governance) and only recorded
as its own trip with the honest **Trip type**. Both non-business types
show amber, deliberately: they are the rows finance has to look at, and
the tax value of the whole log rests on them being honest and few. Mixed
trips are two rows.

## Forgot to log?

It happens. Log it as soon as you remember, reconstruct the odometer from
the surrounding trips — the **By vehicle** view groups every trip under
its car, newest first, so the gap is visible — and say "logged late" in
Notes. The gap-with-honesty rule applies here as everywhere: a marked late
entry is fine; a fabricated tidy one is not.

## The other two views

- **My trips** — everything you've driven, newest first. It shows you
  yours and shows your colleague theirs; it is one view, not one per
  driver.
- **Last 30 days by vehicle** — grouped by car, for the fleet review.
  Note that it shows each trip's kilometres, not a per-car total; the
  totals are an export (see governance).
