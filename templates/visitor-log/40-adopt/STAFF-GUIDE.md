# Visitor log — staff guide

## Signing someone in (reception or the host — 30 seconds)

1. **VI_Visit** → **New** (or the visitor uses the kiosk tablet).
2. Name, organisation, **type**, **host** (the staff member responsible
   for them), purpose. **SignedInAt** = now.
3. **Contractors and students**: tick *Induction sighted* only after
   actually sighting it — the tick is the record that site induction
   happened before work started. No induction, no work; call the host.
4. Badge if you issue them; badge ref on the row.

## Signing out — the half everyone forgets

When your visitor leaves, set **SignedOutAt**. Hosts: your guests are
yours to sign out; reception can't see them leave. The *Never signed out*
view gets tidied each morning — a phantom visitor on the muster list is a
warden searching a building for someone who left at 3 pm yesterday.

## The evacuation moment (wardens)

At the assembly point, open **On site now** on any phone: that's the
muster list — name, host, type. Check people off against reality; the
host of anyone unaccounted-for is standing next to you and knows where
they were headed. This is the sixty seconds the whole register exists for
— and it's why the sign-out habit matters every ordinary day.

## What NOT to do

- Don't sign in groups as "Smith + 4" — every person is a row; the muster
  list can't check off a plus-four.
- Don't record more than the register needs — no visitor phone numbers or
  personal details beyond name and organisation (see the privacy rules in
  governance).
