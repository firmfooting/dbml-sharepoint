# Opportunities register — staff and steward guide

This is a small front door for a project-discovered problem, not another place
to manage work. Capture once; the steward routes once.

## Stop first

Do not use this register as the first report of:

- current or potential patient or staff harm;
- a clinical incident, near miss or open-disclosure matter;
- a complaint;
- a privacy, cyber or information-security concern;
- a statutory breach, emergency or service interruption;
- an issue, risk or change affecting current project delivery.

Use the approved pathway linked at the top of the form. If there is still a
wider out-of-scope improvement to preserve afterwards, return with only a
de-identified process summary and the controlled record ID. Never enter a
patient name, MRN, date of birth, clinical-record content or sensitive staff
detail. Attachments are disabled; link to an authorised source rather than
trying to work around that control. Comments remain available for short
clarifications under the same no-identifiers rule.

## Project teams: submit in about a minute

Open `OR_Opportunity` → **New** and complete five required controls:

1. **Safety and privacy check** — choose the safe-to-record option only when the
   required urgent/sensitive pathway has been used and the content is safe for
   this register. The other option deliberately refuses save.
2. **Problem or opportunity** — one specific line, such as “Reduce repeated
   interpreter-booking checks”, not “Interpreter issue”.
3. **Problem Statement** — in two to four sentences say what happens, who is
   affected and why it matters. Describe the problem, not a preferred system.
4. **Service or facility area** — choose the best first routing destination,
   or **Not sure** rather than guessing.
5. **Source Project** — name the project that encountered it.

Add an Evidence/source link, Project link or scope-boundary sentence when they
are already at hand. Do not search for them merely to submit; the steward can
confirm the boundary at triage. Save. Created By becomes the contact, Status
becomes Captured and Identified Date fills automatically.

Submitters can read only their own records and cannot edit after save. Use the
item comments for a short correction, clarification or answer to a steward.
Never use a comment to add sensitive content or grant someone access: cancel an
`@mention` prompt that offers to share the item. The steward records any
material corrected fact or decision in the governed fields.

## Stewards: use the shortest valid route

Work **Needs triage** oldest first. Most rows should take under three minutes:

1. Recheck the safety/privacy boundary. If a required pathway was missed,
   contact the submitter immediately and route it before doing anything else.
2. Confirm **Why the project cannot own it**. Redirect in-scope work to project
   control rather than legitimising scope drift.
3. Search the obvious destination for an existing record.
4. Choose one **Triage Route** and finish the shortest path below.

| Triage Route | Complete | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Clinical incident/safety, risk, complaint/open disclosure, privacy/cyber, or project issue/change | Receiving system ID/link + Delivery Route | Transferred |
| Direct hand-off to known owner | Owner acceptance + receiving work item/link + Delivery Route | Transferred |
| Duplicate/already governed | Existing record ID/link | Duplicate |
| Close after screening | One concise Decision Rationale | Not proceeding |
| Assess here | Opportunity Owner + one concrete Next Action and due date | Assessing |

Do not populate benefit, effort and measure fields for a redirect, duplicate,
direct hand-off or obvious screening closure. Empty assessment fields are the
proof that unnecessary governance was avoided, not a quality defect.

SharePoint checks that non-Captured records have a triage route and confirmed
scope boundary. It cannot fully enforce that every Status/Triage Route pair is
semantically correct, so check the table above before save.

## Assess only where a real decision remains

For `Assess here`, gather just enough evidence to decide the next destination:

- **Benefit Type** — the primary value;
- **Benefit Potential** — credible reach: Limited, Material, Major or
  System-wide;
- **Time Criticality** — when delay reduces value, not an emergency rating;
- **Effort Band** — Small, Medium or Large before decision; Unknown is honest
  while Assessing;
- **Strategic Alignment** — a named objective/obligation or No direct
  alignment;
- **Patient, consumer and equity consideration** — one line, with detailed
  engagement left to the receiving process;
- **Proposed Benefit Measure** — a practical baseline to target hypothesis;
- **Safety, equity or authority override** — use the existing authorised
  priority whenever one applies.

The routine score is Benefit Potential × Time Criticality. It ranks a
conversation only. Any override blanks the number and shows **Use existing
priority**; follow the organisation's clinical, risk, equity, continuity or
delegation framework instead.

Link a business case or controlled evidence rather than copying it into this
record.

## Decide through existing authority

Set Status to **Awaiting decision**, name the existing Decision Maker/forum and
set a realistic Decision Due Date. Prefer asynchronous approval when the
delegation and evidence are clear. Use an existing meeting only when material
funding, risk, cross-service trade-offs or contested authority require it.

Outcomes:

- **Accepted** — record Decision Date/Rationale, choose Delivery Route and set
  one dated hand-off action. Acceptance is not delivery.
- **Parked** — use sparingly. Record the decision, a Review Due Date and what
  evidence/event will be different then.
- **Not proceeding** — state the concise reason. A full assessment is not
  mandatory merely to record an honest no.

## Hand off and stop

Before `Transferred`, confirm that the receiving owner accepts the item, then
record its ID and link. The receiving system owns delivery and benefit
realisation. Do not copy its progress back into this register.

Use the operational views:

- **Needs triage** — untriaged intake only;
- **Assessments** — commissioned assessment only;
- **Decisions** — matters waiting for existing authority;
- **Handoff and deferred** — accepted transfers and dated deferrals;
- **Closed and redirected** — destination memory and concise closure reasons.

If a view has nothing in it, that is success. Do not create work to keep the
register busy.
