# Declarations register — staff guide

*The rule of thumb for both registers: if you're wondering whether to
declare, declare. Nobody was ever criticised for a declaration; careers end
over the absence of one.*

## Declaring an interest (2 minutes)

Declare when your private interests **could appear** to intersect with your
duties — appearance is the test, not your own confidence in your integrity.
New role on a board? Partner's business tenders to us? Relative in your
reporting line? Declare it.

1. **DR_Interest** → **New**.
2. **Title**: the interest in one neutral line.
3. **Type**, **Declared date** (today), and **Detail**: the facts of the
   interest and where it could touch your role. Don't self-assess — that's
   the coordinator's job with your manager.
4. Save. You can't edit it afterwards; if the interest changes materially,
   submit a fresh declaration and tell the coordinator the old one has
   evolved.

**When the interest ends** (you leave the board, the company is sold), tell
the coordinator — they'll record the cessation.

## Declaring a gift, benefit or hospitality (1 minute)

Offered anything by someone who deals with the organisation — a bottle, a
dinner, tickets, travel? **Declare it whether you accepted or not** (yes,
declined offers too — patterns of offers are exactly what the register
exists to see).

1. **DR_GiftBenefit** → **New**: what, who offered it, when, estimated
   value, context.
2. Above the threshold in the governance rules? Don't accept until the
   decision comes back — "Pending decision" exists for precisely that gap.

## The annual attestation

Once a year you'll be asked to confirm your declarations are current. It's
a two-minute read of your own rows — the *Live interests* view filtered to
you — not a form-filling project. That's the payoff of declaring as you go.
